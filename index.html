03 / About

I work where sensing becomes action.

I’m drawn to robots that must operate with imperfect information: deforming tissue, noisy images, uncertain terrain, and changing real-world constraints.

My practice moves between research and implementation. I enjoy turning ideas into complete systems: calibrating the sensor, shaping the model, closing the control loop, and validating the behavior on hardware.
